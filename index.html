03 / The building

Why 777 Tower?

34.0485° N  118.2614° W  ·  777 S Figueroa St, Los Angeles, CA 90017

[01] BUILDING

Completed in 1991 and designed by AIA Gold Medalist César Pelli, 777 Tower rises above South Figueroa Street with expansive floor plates, considered service, and a landmark marble-clad lobby.

Every floor is designed for flexibility — from single-tenant full floors to adaptable suites — while dedicated parking and round-the-clock security support tenants and guests alike.

[02] LOCATION

2 minto 7th St/Metro
97Walk Score
100Transit Score

777 Tower holds a commanding position at the heart of DTLA's financial core. The building's west side connects directly to the Harbor Freeway (110), with fast on-ramps to the 10, 101, and 5 freeway corridors linking tenants across the region.

Just a two-minute walk from the 7th St/Metro Center station — a hub for multiple Metro rail and light rail lines — the address offers effortless connections to LAX, San Diego, Orange County, Irvine, and the Walt Disney Concert Hall. A 97 Walk Score and a perfect 100 Transit Score make 777 Tower one of the most connected addresses in the city.

[03] CITY

777 Tower sits at the epicenter of Downtown LA's financial core, where global capital and industry leaders converge. Neighbors including Wells Fargo, Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, and JPMorgan Chase create powerful business synergies, while proximity to AT&T's technology hub and USC's talent pipeline keeps tenants close to innovation and rising talent alike.

Beyond business, the address delivers a complete urban lifestyle — the Wilshire Grand, The Broad, and Crypto.com Arena are all within walking distance, uniting premier office space with culture, arts, and city living.
